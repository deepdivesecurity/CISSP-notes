# CISSP - Domain 7: Security Operations

## Digital Forensics Investigation
- Appoint evidence custodian
- Maintain chain of custody
- Use backups
- Make copies of all original evidence
- Chain of evidence
    - Step-by-step timeline of the location, movement, and activities involving a person or a piece of evidence (don’t confuse with chain of custody)

## Incident Response
- NIST 800-61
    - Containment
    - Eradication
    - Recovery
- ISO 27035
    - Responses
- Remediation
    - Can occur at any of the previously mentioned steps
- Reporting
    - Once endorsed by management, SOC transitions to post-incident steps
- Review & improvement

## Root Cause Analysis Techniques
- Pareto analysis
    - 80% of value from 20% of effort
- Five why’s
    - Why did this happen?
- Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagrams
- Failure mode effects analysis
- Fault trees
    - Boolean logic to identify failures

## Business Continuity Planning & Disaster Recovery
- NIST SP 800-34
    - Contingency planning for federal information systems
- ISO 22301
    - Applicable to any org for business continuity management systems (BCMS)
- Business impact analysis (BIA)
- Test BCPs at least annually or when significant change occurs
