## User Habits
- Security training programs should include:
    - Password security practices
    - Clean desk policies and data handling practices
    - NDAs
    - Physical security controls (e.g. badges, tailgating)
    - BYOD policies
    - Organization's acceptable use policy
    - Appropriate use of social media and peer-to-peer networks
