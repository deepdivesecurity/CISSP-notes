# Business Continuity Notes

## Business Continuity Planning
- Business continuity planning is also known as continuity of operations planning (COOP)
- Business continuity is the primary control for supporting the availability objectivty from the CIA triad
- When defining business continuity scope, consider:
    - Business activities
    - Systems
    - Controls
- Business impact assessment (BIA)
    - BIA results are a prioritized list of risks that could disrupt an organization's business
- Business continuity planning in the cloud is a shared responsibility between the CSP and the customer

## Business Continuity Controls
- Redundancy protects against the failure of a single component
- Single point of failure analysis (SPOF)
- IT Contingency planning
- Personnel succession planning

## High Availability & Fault Tolerance
- RAID: Redundant array of inexpensive disks
    - RAID 1: Disk mirroring
    - RAID 5: Disk striping with parity
        - Parity blocks across disks
    - RAID is not a backup strategy
