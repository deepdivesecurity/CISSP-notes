# Understand & Apply Security Concepts (1.2)

## Objectives
Explain confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity, non-repudiation, privacy, and safety.

## Notes
- Confidentiality
    - Addresses unauthorized disclosure of information to someone who does not have a valid need to know.
- Integrity
    - Data is kept free from unauthorized modifications throughout its lifecycle.
- Availability
    - Data is available when needed, where it is needed, and in the form that it is needed.
- Authenticity
    - Whether the people or processes that created & modified data had the permission to do so.
    - Asserts that those creators and modifiers are trustworthy, reliable, and verifiable sources of correctness.
    - When combined with availability, asserts that the instance of the information being displayed is issued by a trusted authority.
    - Authenticity assures that the use of information is in accordance with the organization's mission, objectives, values, and ethics.
- Non-Repudiation
    - The ability of a system to prevent, deny, and detect attempts to deny the authorship, integrity, transmission, or modification of information within or leaving a system.
    - Accountability functions: AAA - Authentication, Authorization, & Accounting.
- Privacy
    - 8 OECD basic privacy principles to protect an individual's right to privacy.
        1. Collection Limitation
        2. Data Quality
        3. Purpose Specification
        4. Use Limitation
        5. Security Safeguards
        6. Openness Principle
        7. Individual Participation
        8. Accountability
    - Individual who is related to a set of personal data is referred to as the "data subject"
- Safety
    - Preventing or controlling unwanted or unauthorized harm to persons or property.
